## Runtime and limits

The synthetic P0--P4 demo uses eight channels and a 20-second, 100 Hz record;
it is intended to finish in seconds. It has no 1728-channel simulation, Monte
Carlo study, CFD, Abaqus, optimization, or formal research result.

## Known limitations

The supplied weights are synthetic. A local pressure harmonic does not by
itself quantify modal loading, and a harmonic comparison does not establish
that the structural response was generated by structural nonlinearity.

## Required real-data metadata

Needed intake metadata are synchronized `time`, `channel_id`, `x/y/z`,
`pressure`, units, sampling frequency, wind speed/direction, tilt angle,
reference velocity/pressure, air density, calibration and filtering details,
plus sensor/tap or tributary areas where known. A generalized-force result also
needs documented mode weights or another stated mapping method and an explicit
permission statement covering the intended analysis.
